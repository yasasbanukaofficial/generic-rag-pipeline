BATTLEX PLATFORM - GOVERNANCE, EMERGENCY CONTROLS AND SUPPORT OPERATIONS

EMERGENCY PLATFORM SWITCHES (KILL-SWITCHES)
To protect platform solvency, player assets, and integrity during unexpected exploits,
critical infrastructure failures, or market volatility, BATTLEX provides platform-wide
emergency switches:

The Core Switches:
   `staked_games_enabled`:
      When disabled: Halts all staked matchmaking queues and prevents new staked lobbies.
      Ongoing games finish normally and settle; Free Play games remain 100% operational.
   `withdrawals_enabled`:
      When disabled: Blocks new withdrawal submissions and prevents signing/broadcasting
        of outbound TRX transfers.
   `merchant_operations_enabled`:
      When disabled: Suspends merchant sell-to-player transfers and buy-back authorizations.
   `tron_deposits_enabled`:
      When disabled: Suspends automated crediting of incoming on-chain TRX deposits.
   `tron_withdrawals_enabled`:
      When disabled: Freezes automated TRX signing and transfers.
   `tron_sweeps_enabled`:
      When disabled: Pauses automated sweeps from the hot wallet to cold storage.

Asymmetric Authorization Model (Fast-Halt, Strict-Resume):
   Emergency Halt (Single Admin): A single super admin can immediately disable any switch
     by providing an audited incident reason. This ensures instantaneous reaction to threats.
   Resumption (Two Super Admins): Re-enabling a switch requires TWO distinct super admins
     with recent session TOTP verification, preventing rogue or compromised staff from
     prematurely lifting security locks.


DYNAMIC ECONOMY CONFIGURATION PROPOSALS
Platform economic parameters (fee waterfall percentages, merchant promotion volume thresholds,
risk alert limits, soft launch parameters) are NOT hardcoded in code. They are managed
via versioned economy proposals:

Maker-Checker Economy Proposals:
   Propose: A super admin creates a proposal derived from the active version, altering
     specific values (e.g. adjusting marketing rate or merchant stage thresholds).
   Minimum 1-Hour Future Effective Time: Proposals must specify an `effective_from` timestamp
     at least 1 hour in the future. Immediate retroactive modifications are strictly prohibited.
   Approval: A DIFFERENT super admin with TOTP reviews the structured JSON diff and approves.
   Immutable Versioning: Historical economy versions are immutable. Completed game settlements
     always retain the specific configuration version that was active at the moment the game concluded.


REAL-TIME INCIDENT ALERTS & NOTIFICATIONS
BATTLEX features a multi-channel operational alerting system:

Alert Triggers:
   `ledger_mismatch`: Continuous reconciliation detected a zero-sum or balance discrepancy.
   `settlement_outbox_stalled`: Game settlement outbox item uncompleted after retry threshold.
   `tron_hot_wallet_low`: Hot wallet balance dropped below `hot_min_sun`.
   `tron_hot_wallet_high`: Hot wallet balance exceeded `hot_max_sun` without successful sweep.
   `tron_transfer_failed`: Outbound TRX withdrawal encountered an unexpected node failure.
   `large_mint_requested`: Mint request exceeding standard threshold.
   `emergency_switch_changed`: Any kill-switch was toggled.

Multi-Channel Dispatch:
   Admin UI Banner: High-priority banner displayed across all admin portal screens.
   Staff Notification Bell & Badges: Real-time badge counts and notification items
     delivered via WebSocket (`staff.notification`) to active admins.
   SMTP Email Alerts: Outbound automated emails dispatched to designated on-call operator emails.


PLAYER CUSTOMER SUPPORT DESK
BATTLEX includes a fully integrated, in-app support ticketing desk (`internal/domains/support`):

Ticket Categories:
   `account`: Login issues, profile data, credentials.
   `deposit_wallet`: Missing merchant or TRON crypto deposits.
   `withdrawal`: Cash-out status, delays, bank info queries.
   `kyc`: Document rejection inquiries, re-submission assistance.
   `game_dispute`: Disconnects, rules disputes, suspected cheating.
   `merchant_issue`: Disagreements or communication issues with merchants.
   `other`: General questions and feedback.

Ticket Status Lifecycle:
   `open`: Newly created ticket awaiting initial staff response.
   `pending_player`: Staff replied; ticket awaiting response from the player.
   `resolved`: Staff resolved the issue and marked the ticket completed.
   `closed`: Player confirmed resolution, or ticket auto-closed after 7 days of inactivity.
   Reopen Window: Resolved or closed tickets can be reopened by the player within 14 days.

Staff Assignment & Collision Detection:
   Admins can assign tickets to themselves or colleagues.
   Optimistic Locking: Staff actions include `expected_assignee_id`. If another admin
     assigns or replies to the ticket concurrently, the collision is caught with a 409 Conflict,
     preventing duplicate or contradictory customer replies.

Internal Notes & Escalations:
   Internal Staff Notes: Staff can post internal notes on a ticket thread. These notes
     are strictly hidden from the player and only visible to authorized administrators.
   Escalation: Staff can escalate complex or high-value financial disputes to super admins.
     Once escalated, only super admins can reply or reassign the ticket.

Canned Responses and Metrics:
   Super admins can manage reusable canned response templates to expedite answers to common questions.
   Operational Metrics: 30-day reporting tracking open tickets, unassigned queue count,
     median first response time, and median time to resolution.
