================================================================================
BATTLEX PLATFORM - GAMEPLAY RULES & ENGINE: CHECKERS (DRAUGHTS)
================================================================================

1. GAME OVERVIEW AND BOARD SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Checkers (American Checkers / English Draughts) is a two-player, purely strategic
board game. In BATTLEX, checkers rules strictly enforce mandatory captures and
kinging mechanics.

A. Board Geometry and Square Numbering:
   - Played on an 8x8 checkerboard (64 squares).
   - Only the 32 dark squares are used. The light squares are never occupied.
   - Standard notation numbers the 32 playable dark squares from 1 to 32:
     * Black (dark pieces) starts on squares 1 through 12.
     * White (light pieces) starts on squares 21 through 32.
     * Squares 13 through 20 represent the middle neutral rows.
   - The board is oriented such that each player has a dark square in the near-left corner.

B. Players & Seating:
   - Exactly 2 players:
     * Seat 1: Black (`seat_1`) - moves first.
     * Seat 2: White (`seat_2`) - moves second.
   - Starting Equipment: 12 discs (men) per player.


2. PIECE MOVEMENT RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Regular Checkers (Men):
   - Move diagonally forward 1 step into an unoccupied dark square.
   - Men can NEVER move backwards.
   - Black moves forward toward squares 21-32; White moves forward toward squares 1-12.

B. Jumping and Capturing (Mandatory Capture Rule):
   - A piece captures an opponent's adjacent piece by jumping over it diagonally into
     the immediately vacant square beyond.
   - The jumped opponent piece is immediately removed from the board.
   - Mandatory Jump: If a player has one or more legal jump options available on their turn,
     they CANNOT make a simple diagonal move. They MUST jump!
   - Choice of Jump: If multiple different jump paths are available, the player may choose
     which jump to take.
   - Multi-Jump Sequences:
     * If a piece completes a jump and can immediately make another jump from its landing square,
       it MUST continue jumping in the same turn.
     * The turn does not conclude until all consecutive jumps in that chain are completed.

C. Crowning / Kinging:
   - When a man reaches the farthest row on the opponent's side (the "King Row": squares 29-32
     for Black; squares 1-4 for White), it is crowned and becomes a "King".
   - In BATTLEX, the board displays a crowned checker graphic.
   - King Powers:
     * A King can move diagonally forward AND diagonally backward 1 square.
     * A King can jump and capture diagonally forward AND diagonally backward.
   - Kinging Turn Stop: If a regular man jumps into the King Row, its turn ends immediately
     upon being crowned. It cannot continue jumping backwards in that same turn as a new king.


3. VICTORY, LOSS AND DRAW CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Victory / Loss Conditions:
   - A player wins when:
     1. Total Capture: They capture all 12 of the opponent's pieces.
     2. Blockade: The opponent has pieces remaining on the board, but has NO legal moves
        available on their turn (all remaining pieces are completely trapped).
   - In staked games, the winner receives 90% of the total pot.

B. Draw Conditions (0% Fee, Full Stake Refund):
   A Checkers match ends in a Draw under two automated conditions:
   1. The 40-Move Rule (80 Plies without Capture):
      - If 80 consecutive plies (40 full moves by each player) occur without a single piece
        capture, the game is automatically declared a Draw.
   2. Threefold Repetition of Position:
      - If the exact same board configuration (identical pieces on identical squares with
        the same player to move) occurs 3 times during the match.

C. Resignation & Forfeits:
   - A player may resign at any time, conceding the win to the opponent.
   - Disconnecting beyond the grace window results in an automated forfeit.


4. ENGINE IMPLEMENTATION & CLIENT PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Server Engine: Located in `internal/domains/engine/checkers`.
- Board Representation: Fixed array of 32 square states:
  * `0`: Empty square
  * `1`: Black Man
  * `2`: Black King
  * `-1`: White Man
  * `-2`: White King
- Action Submission Payload:
  Clients can submit moves specifying square indices (1-32) or a multi-jump path:
  ```json
  {
    "type": "move",
    "payload": {
      "path": [9, 18, 27]
    }
  }
  ```
- Strict Rule Validation: The server validates whether the piece belongs to the active turn,
  whether the path is diagonal, whether landing squares are vacant, and whether mandatory jumps
  are being obeyed. Any non-compliant action is rejected with `ErrIllegalAction`.
