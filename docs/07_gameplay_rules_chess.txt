================================================================================
BATTLEX PLATFORM - GAMEPLAY RULES & ENGINE: CHESS
================================================================================

1. GAME OVERVIEW AND BOARD SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chess is a classic, two-player, purely deterministic game of strategy and tactics.
In BATTLEX, the chess engine enforces standard FIDE international chess rules.

A. Board Dimensions and Coordinates:
   - 8x8 grid consisting of 64 alternating light and dark squares.
   - Files (columns) are labeled `a` through `h` (left to right from White's perspective).
   - Ranks (rows) are numbered `1` through `8` (`1` is White's back rank; `8` is Black's back rank).
   - Coordinate notation: e.g. `e4`, `a1`, `h8`.

B. Players & Seating:
   - Exactly 2 players:
     * Seat 1: White (`seat_1`) - moves first.
     * Seat 2: Black (`seat_2`) - moves second.
   - Starting Pieces per player:
     * 1 King
     * 1 Queen
     * 2 Rooks
     * 2 Bishops
     * 2 Knights
     * 8 Pawns


2. PIECE MOVEMENT RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. King:
   - Moves exactly 1 square in any direction (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).
   - May never move into a square attacked by an opponent's piece (cannot move into check).

2. Queen:
   - Moves any number of unoccupied squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
   - Combines the powers of a Rook and a Bishop. Cannot jump over pieces.

3. Rook:
   - Moves any number of unoccupied squares horizontally or vertically along ranks and files.
   - Cannot jump over pieces.

4. Bishop:
   - Moves any number of unoccupied squares diagonally.
   - Each player begins with one light-squared bishop and one dark-squared bishop,
     which remain on their respective square colors for the entire game.

5. Knight:
   - Moves in an "L-shape": 2 squares in one cardinal direction, then 1 square perpendicular
     (or 1 square cardinal, then 2 squares perpendicular).
   - The Knight is the ONLY chess piece that can jump over other pieces (both friendly and enemy).

6. Pawn:
   - Moves forward vertically 1 square into an unoccupied square.
   - Initial 2-Square Advance: On its very first move, a pawn has the option to advance
     forward 2 squares, provided both squares are unoccupied.
   - Capturing: Pawns capture 1 square diagonally forward. They cannot capture moving straight forward.


3. SPECIAL CHESS MOVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Castling (Kingside & Queenside):
   - A dual move involving the King and one Rook.
   - Kingside (Short) Castling: King moves 2 squares toward the h-file rook; Rook jumps to the f-file.
   - Queenside (Long) Castling: King moves 2 squares toward the a-file rook; Rook jumps to the d-file.
   - Strict Castling Conditions:
     1. Neither the King nor the chosen Rook has moved previously in the game.
     2. All squares between the King and the Rook must be vacant.
     3. The King must NOT currently be in check.
     4. The King must NOT pass through or land on any square attacked by an enemy piece.

B. En Passant:
   - If an opponent advances a pawn 2 squares from its starting square, landing adjacent
     (same rank, neighboring file) to a player's pawn, the player has the option to capture
     that pawn "in passing".
   - The capturing pawn moves diagonally forward to the square the enemy pawn just skipped over.
   - En Passant MUST be played on the immediate move following the 2-square pawn push,
     otherwise the right to capture en passant is permanently lost.

C. Pawn Promotion:
   - When a pawn advances to the opponent's back rank (8th rank for White, 1st rank for Black),
     it cannot remain a pawn.
   - It is immediately promoted to the player's choice of Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
   - Promotion is not limited to previously captured pieces (a player may have multiple Queens).


4. CHECK, CHECKMATE AND GAME TERMINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Check:
   - When a King is under direct attack by one or more enemy pieces.
   - The checked player must immediately escape check by:
     1. Moving the King to an unattacked square.
     2. Capturing the attacking piece.
     3. Blocking the line of attack by placing a friendly piece between the attacker and King
        (not possible against Knight checks).

B. Checkmate (Win / Loss):
   - Occurs when a King is in check and has NO legal moves to eliminate the check.
   - The attacking player wins the game immediately (`status: "checkmate"`).
   - In staked games, the winner receives 90% of the total pot.

C. Draws (0% Fee, Full Refund):
   A game ends in a Draw under the following official conditions:
   1. Stalemate: The player whose turn it is has NO legal moves, but their King is NOT in check.
   2. Threefold Repetition: The exact same board position (same pieces, same turns, same castling
      and en passant rights) occurs 3 times throughout the game.
   3. 50-Move Rule: 50 consecutive moves per side (100 plies) have occurred without any pawn advance
      and without any piece capture.
   4. Insufficient Material: Neither player has sufficient pieces to theoretically force checkmate
      (e.g. King vs King, King & Bishop vs King, King & Knight vs King).
   5. Mutual Draw Agreement: Both players agree to declare a draw.

D. Resignation:
   - A player can click the "Resign" button at any point during their turn or the opponent's turn.
   - The resigning player forfeits the game; the opponent is awarded the victory.

E. Disconnection and Timeouts:
   - If a player runs out of turn time without moving, a timeout penalty is registered.
   - If a player disconnects and fails to reconnect within the 45-60 second grace period,
     they are forfeited, and the remaining player wins.


5. ENGINE IMPLEMENTATION & CLIENT PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Server Engine: Integrated with standard FIDE-compliant chess engine (`internal/domains/engine/chess`).
- Full State Serialization: The server persists the initial FEN plus the complete array of UCI moves
  (`moves: ["e2e4", "e7e5", "g1f3", ...]`) to guarantee 100% fidelity on rehydration and repetition checks.
- Move Payload: Client submits actions formatted in UCI notation:
  ```json
  {
    "type": "move",
    "payload": {
      "from": "e2",
      "to": "e4",
      "promotion": ""
    }
  }
  ```
- Validation: Server validates legality before applying. Invalid moves reject with an error
  message without modifying state or advancing turn clocks.
