================================================================================
BATTLEX PLATFORM - GAMEPLAY RULES & ENGINE: LUDO
================================================================================

1. GAME OVERVIEW AND BOARD STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ludo is a multiplayer cross-and-circle strategy board game derived from ancient
Pachisi. In BATTLEX, Ludo supports 2, 3, or 4 players, played with randomized,
cryptographically secure server-authoritative dice.

A. The Ludo Board Anatomy:
   - 4 Colored Corner Quadrants (Yards): Red, Green, Yellow, Blue.
   - Perimeter Track: 52 shared squares that wrap around the outside of the cross arms.
   - Home Columns (Home Stretch): 4 private colored paths (5 squares each) leading to the center.
   - Center Goal (Home): Square index 57 where finished tokens rest.
   - Safe Squares (8 Safe Zones total):
     * The 4 Starting Squares (where tokens enter the board from the Yard).
     * The 4 Star Squares located halfway along each arm of the cross.
     * Tokens resting on any Safe Square CANNOT be captured by opponent tokens!

B. Player Seats and Colors:
   - 2-Player Match: Opposing seats (e.g. Red vs Yellow, or Green vs Blue).
   - 3-Player Match: 3 active seats.
   - 4-Player Match: Red, Green, Yellow, Blue all active.
   - Each seated player controls 4 tokens of their respective color.


2. THE TURN CYCLE & PHASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each turn in BATTLEX Ludo progresses through clear authoritative phases:

Phase 1: `awaiting_roll`
- The player whose turn it is must click the virtual Die on the UI (or client submits
  `{"type": "roll"}`).
- The server generates a random integer from 1 to 6 using cryptographically secure random bytes
  (`crypto/rand`).
- The die value is broadcast to all players in the room.

Phase 2: `awaiting_move`
- The player must choose which of their 4 tokens to advance by the rolled amount.
- Client submits `{"type": "move", "payload": {"token": <0-3>}}`.
- If only one token can legally move, the client or server auto-selects it to accelerate play.
- Auto-Advance on No Legal Moves: If none of the player's tokens can legally move
  (e.g. all tokens are in Yard and the roll is not a 6), the engine automatically
  concludes the turn and passes the die to the next player.


3. CORE LUDO MOVEMENT RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Releasing Tokens from the Yard:
   - Tokens start in the Yard (Position: `-1`).
   - A token can ONLY exit the Yard onto the starting square (Position: `0` relative to player)
     when the player rolls a 6!
   - Rolls of 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 cannot release a token from the Yard.

B. The Powers and Penalties of Rolling a 6:
   - Rolling a 6 grants an immediate BONUS ROLL!
   - After moving a token, the player retains their turn and rolls the die again.
   - Three Consecutive Sixes Penalty:
     * If a player rolls a 6 three times in a row, the third 6 is penalized.
     * The third roll is immediately cancelled, any move from it is voided, and the turn
       instantly passes to the next player!

C. Capturing Opponent Tokens:
   - If a player's token lands on an occupied square on the shared track:
     * If the square is occupied by an OPPONENT token and is NOT a Safe/Star square:
       The opponent's token is CAPTURED!
       The captured token is knocked off the board and sent back to its owner's Yard (`-1`).
       The capturing player is rewarded with an immediate BONUS DIE ROLL!
     * If the square is a Safe Square:
       Both tokens coexist peacefully on the same square; no capture occurs.
     * Friendly Tokens: Friendly tokens of the same color can occupy the same square.

D. Home Column and Exact Roll to Finish:
   - After completing a full circuit around the 52 perimeter squares, a token turns into
     its color's private Home Column (Squares 52 through 56).
   - Opponent tokens cannot enter another player's private Home Column.
   - Finishing (Square 57):
     * To reach the final center Home (Square 57), the player must roll the EXACT number required.
     * Example: If a token is on Square 55, it needs a roll of exactly 2 to finish.
     * If the player rolls a 4, that token cannot move and the roll cannot be applied to it.


4. VICTORY CONDITIONS AND SINGLE-WINNER PAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Winning Condition:
   - The first player to successfully navigate all 4 of their tokens into the center Goal (Square 57)
     immediately wins the game!
   - The match concludes the instant the 4th token enters Home.

B. Single-Winner Economics (Winner Takes Net Pot):
   - In BATTLEX, all staked Ludo games operate on a single-winner payout model:
     * Winner takes 90% of the entire pot.
     * 10% is deducted as the platform commission fee.
     * All non-winning players (2nd, 3rd, 4th place) receive ZERO coins.
   - Example (4 Players at 100 coins each):
     * Total Pot = 400 coins.
     * Winner Payout = 360 coins (+260 coins net profit).
     * Platform Fee = 40 coins (distributed via fee waterfall).
     * Losers receive = 0 coins.

C. Resignations and Mid-Game Forfeits:
   - In a 3 or 4-player game, if a player resigns or disconnects beyond the grace window:
     * The forfeiting player's tokens are immediately removed or frozen.
     * The game continues uninterrupted among the remaining active players.
     * The forfeiting player's stake remains locked in the game pot; it is NOT refunded.
     * The eventual winner still receives 90% of the total original pot.


5. ENGINE IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Server Engine: Located in `internal/domains/engine/ludo`.
- Ported from high-performance Rust domain logic, fully deterministic when given an RNG stream.
- Token Coordinates:
  * `-1`: Yard
  * `0` to `51`: Perimeter Shared Track (offset normalized per player seat)
  * `52` to `56`: Colored Home Column
  * `57`: Finished / Goal
- State Serialization: Complete game history and token arrays are persisted, enabling
  instant pause, resumption, and fault-tolerant crash recovery.
