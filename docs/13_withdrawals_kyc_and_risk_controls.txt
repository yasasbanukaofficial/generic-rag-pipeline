================================================================================
BATTLEX PLATFORM - WITHDRAWALS, KYC AND RISK CONTROLS
================================================================================

1. WITHDRAWAL RULES AND FINANCIAL LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BATTLEX supports three primary payout channels for player withdrawals:
1. Native TRON TRX on-chain transfers.
2. Manual USDT-TRC20 cryptocurrency payouts.
3. Manual Bank Wire / Local Fiat transfers.

A. Minimum Payout and Platform Withdrawal Fee:
   - Minimum Withdrawal Amount: 2,000 Coins (equivalent to 20.00 USDT).
   - Flat Platform Withdrawal Fee: 200 Coins (equivalent to 2.00 USDT).
   - The fee is charged IN ADDITION to the requested payout amount.
   - Example:
     * Player requests a withdrawal of 2,000 coins.
     * Total reserved from player balance = 2,000 + 200 = 2,200 coins.
     * Player receives net payout value of 2,000 coins.
     * Platform earns 200 coins fee booked to `fee_revenue`.

B. Address Cooling Period (48-Hour Rule):
   - Whenever a player adds a new withdrawal address/bank account or updates an existing one,
     the destination enters a mandatory 48-hour cooling period.
   - Withdrawals to that address are blocked until the 48 hours have fully expired.

C. Credential Change Hold (24-Hour Rule):
   - Any modification to account security settings (password change or email change)
     triggers an automatic 24-hour platform-wide withdrawal hold for that account.

D. Single Open Withdrawal Invariant:
   - A player may have only ONE open withdrawal request at any given time.
   - A new withdrawal cannot be submitted until the pending request is completed or cancelled.


2. TWO-ADMIN MAKER-CHECKER APPROVAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No single person or automated script can unilaterally withdraw money from the platform.
All external payouts require independent two-admin verification:

A. Step 1 - First Financial Approval (Maker):
   - Staff member with `admin` or `super_admin` role reviews the withdrawal queue.
   - Verifies:
     * Player KYC status is valid.
     * No unresolved fraud flags or risk holds are active.
     * Player has legitimately earned coins through gameplay or merchant buy-back.
   - Action: Submits first approval (`status: "first_approved"`).
   - Player Cancellation Ends: Up until this first approval, the player has the right
     to cancel their pending withdrawal. Once first-approved, player cancellation is blocked.

B. Step 2 - Second Financial Approval & Settlement (Checker):
   - A DIFFERENT administrator from the maker, possessing `super_admin` role, performs
     the final release.
   - The checker must provide a valid session TOTP (two-factor authentication) code.
   - For Manual Bank / USDT: The admin transfers funds off-platform and records the
     external transaction reference number in the system.
   - For TRX: The admin triggers the cryptographic signing and on-chain broadcast.
   - Settlement: The reserved 2,200 coins are burned and booked on the ledger:
       Debit:  `player.withdraw_pending` (-2,200 coins)
       Credit: `treasury`                (+2,000 coins burned)
       Credit: `fee_revenue`             (+200 coins fee booked)


3. KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) VERIFICATION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Mandatory KYC Triggers:
   - Cumulative Lifetime Cash-Out: When a player's cumulative withdrawals (including
     merchant buy-backs) exceed the lifetime threshold (default: 1,000 USDT / 100,000 coins).
   - Risk Flag Escalation: If anti-fraud mechanisms flag an account for suspicious velocity
     or collusion, KYC verification is triggered manually before any payouts can proceed.
   - Affiliate Earner Unlock: Affiliates must complete KYC to unlock earnings from `affiliate_locked`.

B. Document Submission:
   - Players upload identity documents via the web portal:
     * Government-issued photo ID (Passport, National Identity Card, Driver's License).
     * Proof of Address (Utility bill, bank statement issued within the last 90 days).
     * Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP (maximum 5 MB per file).

C. Secure Document Storage:
   - Documents are stored in an authenticated, non-public object store:
     * Cloudinary: Private `raw` authenticated storage bucket under `<FOLDER>/kyc/...`.
     * Local Disk (Dev): Restricted filesystem directories with 0700 file permissions.
   - Public URLs are disabled. When staff review documents, the server streams the file
     securely after verifying admin privileges, logging an immutable audit record of the view.

D. Review and Decisioning:
   - Admin verifies document legibility, name matching, and non-expired dates.
   - Decision is marked `verified` or `rejected` with an explanatory reason.
   - Live socket notice `kyc_decision` is pushed to the player.
   - If verified, any funds in `affiliate_locked` are automatically swept to `available`.


4. ANTI-FRAUD AND RISK DETECTION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BATTLEX operates an automated risk detection engine that flags suspicious behaviors
without imposing disruptive automatic bans. The engine creates reviewable risk flags
for human administrative investigation:

A. Monitored Behavioral Signals:
   1. `repeated_pairs`:
      - Two players repeatedly matching against each other in public queues across
        multiple high-stake matches (suspected win-trading or collusion).
   2. `one_sided_wins`:
      - Abnormal, statistically improbable win streaks where one player consistently
        defeats another specific opponent to siphon funds.
   3. `shared_ip_device`:
      - Multiple accounts competing against each other from the same IP address,
        browser fingerprint, or device signature.
   4. `tron_deposit_no_play`:
      - A player deposits cryptocurrency (TRX) and immediately attempts to cash out
        via merchant or bank transfer without playing staked games (suspected money laundering
        or currency arbitrage).
   5. `disproportionate_payouts`:
      - Payout requests that vastly exceed a player's lifetime game winnings and legitimate deposits.

B. Risk Flag Handling:
   - Detection does NOT freeze gameplay or abruptly ban the user.
   - Instead, if an open risk flag exists for an account submitting a withdrawal:
     * The withdrawal is automatically placed on an administrative "Risk Hold".
     * Administrators must review the flagged evidence, inspect game replay history,
       and verify user identity before releasing the hold.
