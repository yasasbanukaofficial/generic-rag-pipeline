BATTLEX PLATFORM - GAME POT SETTLEMENT AND REVENUE FEE WATERFALL

GAME POT ESCROW & SPLIT RULES
When players enter a staked match (e.g. Chess, Checkers, or Ludo), their stakes are
locked atomically into escrow:

Atomic Escrow Hold:
   At match start, the stake amount is debited from each player's `available` account
     and credited to their `in_play` account:
       Debit:  `player.available` (-Stake)
       Credit: `player.in_play`   (+Stake)
   If any player has insufficient funds at the moment the match forms, the hold fails,
     no players are charged, the underfunded player is ejected, and funded players are
     requeued with original priority.

Pot Sizing:
   Total Pot = Stake per player * Number of seated players
   Examples:
     2 Players at 100 coins each = 200 Coin Pot
     2 Players at 250 coins each = 500 Coin Pot
     4 Players at 100 coins each = 400 Coin Pot

Winning Outcome (90% / 10% Rule):
   The platform charges a 10% commission fee ONLY on games with a clear winner.
   Winner Payout: 90% of the Total Pot.
   Platform Fee: 10% of the Total Pot.
   Example (2-player match at 250 coins):
     Total Pot = 500 coins.
     Winner receives: 500 * 0.90 = 450 coins.
     Platform Fee: 500 * 0.10 = 50 coins.
     Net profit to winner = 450 - 250 (own stake) = +200 coins profit.

Single-Winner Rule in Multi-Player Games (Ludo):
   In 3-player and 4-player Ludo, BATTLEX enforces a winner-takes-all model.
   The first player to finish wins 90% of the entire pot.
   Non-winners (2nd, 3rd, 4th place) receive 0 coins.
   Example (4 players at 100 coins):
     Total Pot = 400 coins.
     Winner receives: 360 coins (+260 coins net profit).
     Platform fee: 40 coins.
     All 3 losers receive 0.

Draws and Voids (0% Fee Guarantee):
   If a game ends in a Draw (e.g. Chess stalemate, threefold repetition, 50-move rule,
     or Checkers draw) or is Voided due to a server fault/timeout:
     Zero platform fee is charged (0%).
     100% of the held stakes are returned from `in_play` to each player's `available` account:
         Debit:  `player.in_play`   (-Stake)
         Credit: `player.available` (+Stake)


THE 100% REVENUE SHARE WATERFALL
The 10% platform fee is treated as gross revenue (100% of the commission) and is
distributed across a predefined waterfall configured in the economy engine:

Total Platform Fee = 100% (e.g. 50 coins from a 500 coin pot)

Distribution Breakdown:
Operations, Growth & Affiliates (45% of Fee):
   Unilevel Affiliate Commissions: 10% of fee (5.0 coins)
     L1 Upline: 4% of fee (2.0 coins)
     L2 Upline: 3% of fee (1.5 coins)
     L3 Upline: 2% of fee (1.0 coins)
     L4 Upline: 1% of fee (0.5 coins)
     Note: Split 50/50 between winner side and non-winner sides.
   Direct Referral Bonus: 5% of fee (2.5 coins)
     Split 50/50 between winner's direct inviter and non-winner inviters.
   Merchant Level Pools: 5.25% of fee (2.625 coins)
     Sub Merchant L1 Pool: 0.5% of fee
     Sub Merchant L2 Pool: 1.0% of fee
     Sub Merchant L3 Pool: 2.0% of fee
     Main Merchant L1 Pool: 0.25% of fee
     Main Merchant L2 Pool: 0.5% of fee
     Main Merchant L3 Pool: 1.0% of fee
   Merchant Discretionary Bonus Pool: 4.75% of fee (2.375 coins)
     Accrues in platform system account for discretionary distribution.
   Marketing Pool: 10% of fee (5.0 coins)
     Credited to platform marketing system account.
   Development & Infrastructure Pool: 10% of fee (5.0 coins)
     Credited to platform dev/infrastructure system account.

Net Profit Pool (55% of Fee + Residuals):
   Base Net Profit: 55% of fee (27.5 coins)
   PLUS Unclaimed Affiliate Shares (e.g. missing uplines, soft-deleted accounts)
   PLUS Micro-coin Rounding Residuals
   PLUS Empty Merchant Level Pools at month close

Net Profit Pool Split:
   Company Treasury: 40% of Net Profit Pool
     At base full coverage: 27.5 * 0.40 = 11.0 coins (22% of total fee)
   Directors Pool: 60% of Net Profit Pool
     At base full coverage: 27.5 * 0.60 = 16.5 coins (33% of total fee)


DIRECTORS REGISTER & MONTHLY STATEMENT CLOSE
Director Register:
   Platform stakeholders (investors) hold registered shares in the platform.
   Shares are measured in basis points totaling exactly 10,000 bps (100.00%).
   Example Default: Four equal directors holding 2,500 bps (25.00%) each.
   Modifications to the director register require maker-checker super admin approval
     and take effect on the first day of the subsequent UTC calendar month.

Monthly Close & Statement Generation:
   On the 1st of each UTC month, an automated reconciliation job calculates the
     period-end Net Profit and Directors Pool balance.
   Each director's statement line is generated based on their basis point share.
   Statement rows round down to integer micro-coins; rounding fractions carry forward.
   Statements are closed immutably with two-admin maker-checker review and TOTP.
   Statements are paid off-platform (bank wire, crypto) and recorded with external
     payment references. Recording a paid statement executes an audited ledger burn:
       Debit:  `directors` (-Amount)
       Credit: `treasury`  (+Amount)
     This permanently settles the platform's financial obligation.
