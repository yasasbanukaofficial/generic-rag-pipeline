================================================================================
BATTLEX PLATFORM - REFERRAL AND AFFILIATE PROGRAM
================================================================================

1. REFERRAL MECHANISM AND BINDING RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BATTLEX features a multi-tier affiliate referral system designed to incentivize
organic player acquisition and community growth.

A. Binding Rules:
   - Registration Binding: When a user registers using an affiliate invite link
     (e.g. `https://battlex.com/register?ref=BX1234`), the referrer is bound
     immediately within the atomic user registration transaction.
   - Post-Registration Grace Period: If a user registers without a referral link,
     they have up to 7 calendar days to manually enter a referral code in their
     profile settings, provided they have NOT yet played any staked games.
   - Strict Immutability: Once a referral link is established, it can never be
     changed, reassigned, or deleted.
   - Loop & Self-Referral Prevention: The system strictly validates that a user
     cannot refer themselves, nor can any circular loops be formed at any depth
     (e.g. A -> B -> C -> A is rejected).


2. COMMISSION STRUCTURE (THE 50/50 WINNER/LOSER SPLIT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commissions are funded out of the platform's 10% game fee (never deducted from the
player's won pot).

A. Two Earning Streams:
   1. Unilevel Affiliate Commissions (10% of total fee):
      - Level 1 (Direct Referrer): 4% of total fee
      - Level 2 (Referrer's Referrer): 3% of total fee
      - Level 3: 2% of total fee
      - Level 4: 1% of total fee
   2. Direct Referral Bonus (5% of total fee):
      - Paid directly to the Level 1 referrers of the participants.

B. The 50/50 Side Allocation:
   In every completed staked game, the commission for each unilevel tier and the
   direct referral bonus is split 50/50 between:
   - 50% allocated to the Winner's upline chain.
   - 50% allocated across the Non-Winner (Loser) upline chains.

   Why this design matters:
   - It rewards affiliates whose invited players play games, regardless of whether
     their player wins or loses!
   - In 2-player games (Chess, Checkers), the non-winner 50% goes entirely to the loser's upline.
   - In multi-seat games (3 or 4-player Ludo), the non-winner 50% is divided equally among
     the uplines of all non-winning players at that level, rounded down to micro-coins.


3. WORKED EXAMPLE (500-COIN MATCH, 50-COIN FEE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Consider a 2-player game with a 500-coin pot (Fee = 50 coins = 50,000,000 micro-coins).
Assuming both players have full 4-level uplines:

Recipient Tier        Winner Side (50%)    Loser Side (50%)    Total Coins
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Level 1 (4% of fee)   1.00 coin            1.00 coin           2.00 coins
Level 2 (3% of fee)   0.75 coins           0.75 coins          1.50 coins
Level 3 (2% of fee)   0.50 coins           0.50 coins          1.00 coin
Level 4 (1% of fee)   0.25 coins           0.25 coins          0.50 coins
Direct Referral (5%)  1.25 coins           1.25 coins          2.50 coins
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Total Affiliate Paid: 3.75 coins           3.75 coins          7.50 coins

Key Takeaway for Direct Referrers (L1):
Each player's direct referrer receives:
  L1 (1.00) + Direct Bonus (1.25) = 2.25 coins (4.5% of the total platform fee)!


4. UNCLAIMED COMMISSIONS AND ROUNDING RESIDUALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Missing Uplines: If a player was not referred by anyone, or their upline chain
  stops at Level 1 or Level 2, the unearned higher-level commissions (L3, L4)
  are NOT retained arbitrarily.
- Soft-Deleted Accounts: If an upline account was soft-deleted, their share is forfeited.
- Policy: All unclaimed affiliate commissions and micro-coin division residuals
  are automatically swept into the Net Profit Pool, thereby increasing company
  and director distributions.


5. AFFILIATE LOCK AND KYC RELEASE WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent referral abuse, multi-account self-farming, and money laundering:

A. The `affiliate_locked` Ledger Account:
   - When a settlement produces affiliate earnings for a user, the system checks
     the recipient's KYC verification status.
   - If the recipient user has NOT completed KYC verification:
     * The earned coins are credited to the user's `affiliate_locked` account.
     * Locked coins cannot be staked in games, transferred to merchants, or withdrawn.

B. Automated Ledger Release upon KYC Approval:
   - As soon as platform administrators verify the user's KYC submission:
     * An automated, idempotent ledger transaction is executed.
     * All accumulated balances in `affiliate_locked` are debited and credited to `available`:
         Debit:  `user.affiliate_locked` (-Balance)
         Credit: `user.available`        (+Balance)
   - Future affiliate earnings for that user immediately route directly to `available`.
   - A background recovery sweep periodically verifies that any newly approved KYC users
     have their locked balances swept without manual operator intervention.
