BATTLEX PLATFORM - MERCHANT SYSTEM AND P2P OPERATIONS

ROLE OF MERCHANTS IN THE ECOSYSTEM
Merchants act as decentralized franchise liquidity partners in the BATTLEX ecosystem.
Because traditional online payment gateways often restrict gaming or operate with
prohibitive fees in certain target jurisdictions, merchants facilitate local fiat
cash-in (deposits) and buy-back (cash-outs) directly with players.

Key Principles:
Pre-Funded Inventory: Merchants must purchase and pre-fund coin inventory via verified
  external bank/crypto deposits before they can sell coins to players.
No Self-Sale: A merchant cannot sell coins to their own user account.
Spread Revenue: Merchants make profit primarily through the off-platform spread
  between what they charge players to buy coins vs what they pay to buy back coins.
Segregated Accounts: A merchant user maintains both a `merchant` inventory account
  and a separate personal `player` account.


THE 7 MERCHANT STAGES & PROGRESSION
Merchants progress through seven recognized operating stages:
Entry Merchant
Sub Merchant Level 1 (Sub L1)
Sub Merchant Level 2 (Sub L2)
Sub Merchant Level 3 (Sub L3)
Main Merchant Level 1 (Main L1)
Main Merchant Level 2 (Main L2)
Main Merchant Level 3 (Main L3)

Stage Progression & Eligibility:
Entry Stage: Earns spread income only. No eligibility for platform pool revenue.
Sub L1 through Main L3: Eligible to receive equal shares of platform-wide merchant level pools.
Promotion Rules:
  Promotions are evaluated automatically at UTC month close based on the merchant's
    total verified sell-to-player volume during that calendar month.
  A merchant can advance at most ONE stage per month.
  No automatic demotion: Once a stage is achieved, the merchant retains it unless
    suspended by administrators for policy violations.


INVENTORY MINTING AND RESTOCKING
Initial Entry Merchant Creation:
   Prospective merchant deposits verified funds (e.g. $150 USD equivalent) to platform operators.
   Admin Maker creates merchant request with external deposit receipt/transaction reference.
   Admin Checker (with TOTP) verifies funds receipt and approves.
   System atomically creates merchant record and mints initial inventory:
       Debit:  `treasury`           (-15,000 coins)
       Credit: `merchant.available` (+15,000 coins)

Restocking Existing Inventory:
   When a merchant's inventory runs low, they repeat the two-admin mint request.
   Alternatively, performing player buy-backs automatically replenishes merchant inventory!


SELL-TO-PLAYER WORKFLOW (CASH-IN)
Step 1: Player contacts merchant (in-person, phone, messaging, or P2P in-app chat)
        and sends local fiat funds (e.g. LKR 3,450 for 1,000 coins).
Step 2: Merchant opens the Merchant Terminal in the web portal.
Step 3: Merchant enters the player's 6-character public Display ID (e.g. "BX7890")
        and the coin amount (1,000).
Step 4: Merchant clicks "Sell Coins".
Step 5: The server executes an atomic, idempotent ledger transfer:
          Debit:  `merchant.available` (-1,000 coins)
          Credit: `player.available`   (+1,000 coins)
Step 6: An immutable digital transaction receipt is generated, recording the merchant ID,
        player ID, amount, timestamp, and active rate version.
Step 7: The player's balance updates instantly; player can immediately stake or play.


BUY-BACK WORKFLOW (CASH-OUT)
To protect players from unauthorized withdrawals or fraudulent merchant claims,
buy-backs require player authorization via password:

Step 1: Player requests cash-out through a merchant.
Step 2: Merchant initiates a Buy-Back Request in the terminal specifying player's
        Display ID and coin amount (e.g. 2,000 coins).
Step 3: The system places the request in `pending_confirmation` status with a strict
        10-minute expiration countdown (TTL).
Step 4: The player receives an on-screen prompt and notification in their portal.
Step 5: The player reviews the merchant name and coin amount, enters their ACCOUNT PASSWORD,
        and clicks "Authorize".
Step 6: The server validates the password, checks the player's daily withdrawal limits
        and KYC status, and transfers the coins:
          Debit:  `player.available`   (-2,000 coins)
          Credit: `merchant.available` (+2,000 coins)
Step 7: The merchant pays the agreed fiat amount to the player off-platform (e.g. bank transfer or cash).

Safety Limits:
Buy-backs count directly toward the player's UTC-day and 30-day withdrawal caps.
If a player's password is submitted incorrectly 3 times, or the 10-minute window expires,
  the buy-back request is automatically aborted.


MONTHLY MERCHANT LEVEL POOLS DISTRIBUTION
As part of the platform's 10% game commission waterfall:
5.25% of all platform game fees accrue across 6 level pools:
  Sub L1 Pool: 0.50% of fee
  Sub L2 Pool: 1.00% of fee
  Sub L3 Pool: 2.00% of fee (shared by Sub L3 and all Main merchants)
  Main L1 Pool: 0.25% of fee
  Main L2 Pool: 0.50% of fee
  Main L3 Pool: 1.00% of fee

Distribution Rules:
Evaluated at the end of every UTC calendar month.
Each pool's accumulated coins are divided EQUALLY among all active, eligible merchants
  at that stage, regardless of individual volume!
Shares round down to integer micro-coins; division residuals roll into the Net Profit Pool.
Empty Pools: If no merchants currently qualify for a specific level, that pool's balance
  is swept into the Net Profit Pool.
Suspended Merchants: A suspended merchant forfeits all pool earnings.


INVENTORY REDEMPTION AND REFUNDS
Inventory Redemption (Merchant Cash-Out):
   A merchant wishing to cash out excess coin inventory submits a Redemption Request.
   Coins are burned on the ledger; the platform pays out fiat/crypto at the base peg:
       Debit:  `merchant.available` (-Coins)
       Credit: `treasury`           (+Coins)
   Requires two-admin maker-checker review with TOTP and recorded payout reference.

Entry Merchant Refund:
   If an Entry Merchant decides to leave the platform before selling their initial inventory,
     they can request a refund.
   Refund calculation: The refund is reduced proportionately for any coins already sold
     to prevent double payment.


P2P MERCHANT CHAT, DIRECTORY & MODERATION
Directory: Promoted active merchants appear in the merchant directory (`/wallet/merchants`),
  displaying their verified payment accounts (Bank details, USDT-TRC20 address).
In-App Chat: Players can open a secure chat thread directly with a merchant to negotiate
  and coordinate fiat bank transfers.
Attachments: Supports JPEG/PNG/WebP bank deposit slips (under 5 MB) stored privately.
Staff Moderation: Platform staff (admins) have full search and monitor visibility across
  all chat threads to prevent fraud, abusive conduct, or chargeback scams.
Staff Actions: Admins can hide illicit messages or threads with an audited reason trail.
